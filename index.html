I · About

A note from Esther

I’m an experienced pet carer specializing in in-home dog and cat minding. Over the last 10 years, I’ve worked deeply in dog behavior and psychology, which helps me understand exactly what pets need to feel calm, safe, and comfortable in their own home while you’re away.

I’m also trained in canine first aid, so you can have complete peace of mind knowing your pets and your home are in safe, capable hands.

To me, good care isn’t about fuss. It’s about keeping to your pets’ routines, reading how they’re feeling, and giving them the exercise, quiet and company they actually need, so that when you come home you find them settled and content.

II · Services

Care that keeps their world the same

Animals feel safest where everything is familiar: their own bed, their own garden, the usual sounds and smells of the day. So rather than moving your pets to kennels or a stranger’s house, I come to them.

Overnight stays

I stay in your home while you’re away, keeping meals, walks and bedtimes just as your pets expect. Someone is with them through the evening and the night, which matters most to older and anxious animals.

Long-term stays

Away for weeks or longer? I settle into your household for the duration, so your pets keep their routine and your home is lived in and looked after while you’re gone.

Medical & special needs

Senior pets, pets on medication, animals recovering from surgery and dogs who find the world overwhelming are where my experience counts most.

III · Expertise

Specialist care, calmly given

Some pets need more than food, water and a walk. I’m experienced in caring for older animals, pets on medication or recovering from surgery, and dogs who find the world overwhelming.

Health & medical needs

  • Medication given on schedule: tablets, liquids, eye and ear drops, and topical treatments
  • Injections such as insulin, as prescribed by your vet
  • Recovery after surgery or illness, following your vet’s aftercare plan
  • Senior pets and chronic conditions, with careful daily monitoring
  • Trained in canine first aid, and calm if something goes wrong

Behavior & wellbeing

  • Anxious, fearful or reactive dogs, handled with patience and understanding
  • Pets who struggle when left alone, with someone in the house overnight
  • Puppies and newly adopted dogs who need structure while they settle
  • Steady routines, the single biggest comfort to an unsettled animal
  • Calm, kind handling grounded in ten years of behavior work

Before every stay we go through your pet’s health, medication and routine together, and I keep your vet’s details to hand.

V · How it works

First, a meet & greet

Every booking starts with a free, no-obligation visit, so your pets can get to know me before I’m left in charge of them.

  1. I

    Get in touch

    Message me on WhatsApp with your dates, where you are, and a little about your pets.

  2. II

    Meet & greet

    I visit you at home, free of charge, to meet your pets on their own ground and learn their routines and needs.

  3. III

    Handover

    Before you leave, we go through feeding, medication, walks, vet details and anything about the house I should know.

  4. IV

    While you’re away

    Your pets keep their routine, and a daily update lets you relax and enjoy your time away.
